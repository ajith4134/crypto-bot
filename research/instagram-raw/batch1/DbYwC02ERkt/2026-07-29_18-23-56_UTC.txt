A viral claim says a quantitative trading bot built with Claude generated $220,000 in 64 days by reacting to breaking news faster than prediction-market prices could adjust.🤯

The system reportedly monitored new information, identified markets with delayed price reactions, and entered positions during the brief gap before other traders responded.

But there is an important warning. The profit figure has not been independently verified, and real-world automated trading involves fees, failed executions, liquidity limits, technical errors, and the risk of major losses.

Claude may help developers analyse information and write code, but it does not turn a strategy into guaranteed profit. Any backtest or viral result should be examined carefully before real money is involved.

The bigger story is how AI is making advanced market analysis and automation more accessible, while also creating new risks for anyone who mistakes speed for certainty.
